Straight Answers.

Is Daaymn free?

Yes. You can create a profile, discover people, match and chat without paying. Extra likes are an optional in-app purchase.

Can you read my messages?

No. Messages are encrypted on your device with a key we never hold. That is a property of how the app is built, not a promise.

Which devices?

Android, on Google Play. The app is built with Flutter, so other platforms are possible later.

How do I delete my account?

From inside the app, or email daaymnco@gmail.com and we will remove it.
